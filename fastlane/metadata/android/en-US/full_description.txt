Polyphony is an open-source Android music player for self-hosted libraries.

• Subsonic, OpenSubsonic, Navidrome and Jellyfin servers; several at once
• A local library cache so browsing and search are instant and work offline
• Gapless playback, ReplayGain, lyrics, sleep timer, scrobbling, equaliser hookup
• Google Cast with seamless hand-off between your phone and the receiver
• Transcoding profiles with rules per network (Wi-Fi / mobile data), per Cast device, and an
  adaptive mode that lowers quality when the connection stalls and restores it afterwards
• Material 3 Expressive design with dynamic colour and artwork-tinted Now Playing

No accounts, no tracking. Your music stays between your device and your server.
